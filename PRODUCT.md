# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript, chosen by the user (Devera Studio). Static-first, deployable to Vercel or any Node host.

## Users

- Primary: homeowners in Slovenia renovating or building a house (balcony, yard, stairs, carport). Often browse in the evening on a phone, compare photos of finished work, and want a rough idea of price before calling.
- Secondary: companies and builders (pravne osebe) ordering custom metal structures, stairs and fences for commercial objects.
- Internal: HIFA staff, who receive inquiries and today waste time collecting measures and photos by phone.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and lead-generation site for HIFA d.o.o., a family metalwork company from Njiverce near Kidričevo (since 1990) that designs, manufactures in its own workshop and installs aluminium, stainless and steel fences, yard gates, canopies, staircases and custom metal structures across Slovenia, and installs Holler DIY aluminium gates.
Success = more complete inquiries (type, measures, photos, location) and fewer back-and-forth phone calls.

## Positioning

Made to measure in their own workshop since 1990, with full in-house chain: measure on site, drawing, fabrication, hot-dip galvanising and/or powder coating in any RAL colour, installation. Robotic welding cell and laser welding (NOO-funded 2022-2023).

## Operating Context

Inquiry flow (confirmed on current site): inquiry with photos/measures/location → informative quote → on-site visit and exact measuring → final quote and project drawings → fabrication → installation.
Current site has two third-party Salesqueze configurators (fences: 4 types, canopies: Box and Enokapnica) that will be replaced by an in-house configurator.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: fences and yard gates (balcony, yard, outdoor stair, indoor stair; sliding, cantilever, swing gates, pedestrian gates, motorised with photocell and signal light), canopies (cars, entrances, terraces, balconies; glass, polycarbonate, trapezoid sheet with insulation, roof foil), staircases (indoor/outdoor, often with wood), custom structures (also installs products not bought from them), Holler DIY (sliding up to 6 x 2 m, swing up to 4.34 x 2 m, drives hidden in the post, CE EN 13241-1, standard colours anthracite/silver/green).
- Materials: aluminium, stainless steel (inox), black steel (hot-dip galvanised + powder coated).
- Old URL slugs must keep working (SEO): /ograje, /nadstreski, /stopnisca, /storitve, /holler-diy, /galerije/*, /kontakt, /o-nas, /proizvodi, /sofinanciranje, /pravno-obvestilo.
- EU co-funding notice (NOO / NextGenerationEU) and its page must remain visible on the site.
- Undecided: real price list for the configurator (prices in this draft are sample values and must be labelled as such), form delivery backend, analytics IDs.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work: real photos of finished projects are the strongest proof.
2. Make inquiring effortless on a phone, with measures and photos attached.
3. Let people play with their fence or canopy before they call.
4. Plain, correct Slovenian; no hype.
5. Fast on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target; zoom must not be disabled; older homeowners are a real audience (large tap targets, readable type).
