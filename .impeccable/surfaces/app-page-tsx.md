## Scope

Home page of hifa.si redesign (Persuade). Product pages, galleries and the configurator inherit this world.

## Audience and job

Slovenian homeowner on a phone in the evening, planning a balcony railing, yard fence and gate, carport canopy or outdoor stairs. Job: see that HIFA does exactly their thing, shape it, and send an inquiry with measures and photos. Proof: ~330 real project photos, 3 real testimonials, since 1990, own workshop.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the front elevation of a Slovenian family house, and every HIFA product on it is a working part you can tap and shape. Refuses the dark-photo hero with a green button over a row of product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Architect's elevation sheet. Cool façade-plaster grey ground, RAL 7016 anthracite as linework and as the metal itself, brand green #91BD30 only for actions and the selected part. Detail bubbles (circle + letter A–D) tie the house to product sections. Archivo wide for headlines, Archivo condensed caps for drawing labels and dimensions. Real photos sit as framed detail plates.

STORY: Visitor recognises their house, taps the part they need, changes infill and RAL colour live, sends an inquiry for that part. Below: one detail section per part with real photos, before/after, process, testimonials, contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: the elevation spans the sheet (width capped so the part strip fits in 900 px), H1 and one proof line set in the empty sky top-right above the carport like lettering on the sheet (moved from a left column so the house keeps full width; decided in review). Hotspots A balcony railing, B carport canopy, C entrance stairs, D yard fence and sliding gate. Under the drawing, the selected-part strip as a title block: infill chips, RAL swatches, primary green "Povpraševanje za ta del". Mobile: drawing first at full width (cropped to house, carport and gate), then H1, then part tabs and options; call and inquiry sticky at the bottom.

FORM: candidate 4 of 7 (house elevation), seed:2fc82e44f6f33b35. Signature interaction: tapping a part highlights it in green linework and redraws its infill and colour live in the elevation; the choice travels into the inquiry form.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
